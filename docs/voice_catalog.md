## Detailed Profile Specifications

### 01. `j_jazz_atelier_pair` (J-Jazz / Anisong Slap Machine)
* **Archetype:** Atelier Z / Sadowsky Tokyo Active Jazz Bass
* **Coil Model:** Two narrow single coils ($w = 0.75''$) in parallel spaced $\sim 3.6''$ apart
* **Electrical Parameters:** $L = 1.69\text{ H}$, $R_{\text{dc}} = 3.69\text{ k}\Omega$, $R_{\text{eddy}} = 65\text{ k}\Omega$, $C_{\text{coil}} = 145\text{ pF}$
* **The Mix Role:** Symmetrical dual-aperture phase cancellation produces a clean, hollow mid-scoop at $1\text{ kHz}$ with extended sparkle up to $5.5\text{ kHz}$. In 180–220 BPM Japanese anime themes and fusion passages, fast slap pops and double-thumps explode through dense brass, guitars, and synths without cluttering the vocal space.
* **Bass Setting:** ABCX Blend at **Center Detent (50/50)**.

### 02. `jaco_fusion_bridge` (J-Fusion / Soloing Bark)
* **Archetype:** 1970s Fender Jazz Bass Bridge Single Coil
* **Coil Model:** Single coil ($w = 0.75''$) positioned $1.6''$ from the bridge
* **Electrical Parameters:** $L = 3.60\text{ H}$, $R_{\text{dc}} = 7.80\text{ k}\Omega$, $R_{\text{eddy}} = 125\text{ k}\Omega$, $C_{\text{coil}} = 70\text{ pF}$
* **The Mix Role:** Tightly focused harmonic bark centered in the $800\text{ Hz}\text{--}1.6\text{ kHz}$ range with rolled-off sub-bass. Pierces through dense keyboards and big band arrangements for rapid staccato fingerstyle, chordal harmonics, and melodic soloing.
* **Bass Setting:** ABCX Blend **100% Bridge** (TWX in Single-Coil Mode).

### 03. `jrock_modern_p` (J-Rock / Anisong Pick Foundation)
* **Archetype:** Bartolini 8CBP Ceramic P-Bass
* **Coil Model:** Ceramic split-coil ($w = 1.00''$)
* **Electrical Parameters:** $L = 4.80\text{ H}$, $R_{\text{dc}} = 9.50\text{ k}\Omega$, $R_{\text{eddy}} = 110\text{ k}\Omega$, $C_{\text{coil}} = 80\text{ pF}$
* **The Mix Role:** Immediate, hard-hitting pick attack with compressed ceramic low-mids ($300\text{--}800\text{ Hz}$) and rolled-off clatter. The standard driving bass engine for modern Japanese rock intros and power chord verses (Radwimps, Asian Kung-Fu Gen).
* **Bass Setting:** ABCX Blend **100% Neck (PX)**.

### 04. `vintage_62_alnico_p` (Organic Classic Rock & Pop)
* **Archetype:** 1962 Fender Precision with Alnico V Magnets
* **Coil Model:** Alnico V split-coil ($w = 1.00''$)
* **Electrical Parameters:** $L = 3.80\text{ H}$, $R_{\text{dc}} = 10.50\text{ k}\Omega$, $R_{\text{eddy}} = 180\text{ k}\Omega$, $C_{\text{coil}} = 60\text{ pF}$
* **The Mix Role:** Lower core eddy-current damping gives an open, uncompressed, touch-sensitive breath with a higher resonant peak ($2.8\text{ kHz}$). Ideal for acoustic singer-songwriter, vintage 70s rock, blues, and ballad arrangements where dynamic nuance is paramount.
* **Bass Setting:** ABCX Blend **100% Neck (PX)**.

### 05. `motown_neo_soul_dub` (Pillowy Sub-Thump)
* **Archetype:** James Jamerson / Pino Palladino (Tone Rolled to 0)
* **Coil Model:** P-Bass split-coil loaded by direct $47\text{ nF}$ shunt
* **Electrical Parameters:** $L = 4.80\text{ H}$, $R_{\text{dc}} = 9.50\text{ k}\Omega$, $C_{\text{tone}} = 47\text{ nF}$
* **The Mix Role:** Strips away all finger attack and string clack above $800\text{ Hz}$, anchoring the track purely in the fundamental $40\text{--}200\text{ Hz}$ register. Essential for neo-soul, reggae, dub, and retro R&B palm-muting.
* **Bass Setting:** ABCX Blend **100% Neck (PX)**.

### 06. `studio_workhorse_pj` (Everyday Rock & Pop)
* **Archetype:** Fender Precision Special / Yamaha BB / Duff McKagan
* **Coil Model:** Split P ($1.0''$) $+$ Bridge Single ($0.75''$) in parallel
* **Electrical Parameters:** $L = 2.06\text{ H}$, $R_{\text{dc}} = 4.28\text{ k}\Omega$, $R_{\text{eddy}} = 60\text{ k}\Omega$, $C_{\text{coil}} = 150\text{ pF}$
* **The Mix Role:** The most recorded studio hybrid tone in modern history. Combines $80\%$ of a P-bass's chest-thumping low end with $20\%$ added bridge bite for finger and pick clarity. Sits effortlessly in almost any commercial mix.
* **Bass Setting:** ABCX Blend at **Center Detent (50/50)** (TWX in Single-Coil Mode).

### 07. `jmetal_prog_stingray` (J-Metal & Modern Djent Weapon)
* **Archetype:** Music Man StingRay 4-string / Dingwall Parallel
* **Coil Model:** Dual-coil humbucker ($w = 1.50''$, $d = 0.75''$) in parallel
* **Electrical Parameters:** $L = 2.40\text{ H}$, $R_{\text{dc}} = 4.60\text{ k}\Omega$, $R_{\text{eddy}} = 140\text{ k}\Omega$, $C_{\text{coil}} = 130\text{ pF}$
* **The Mix Role:** Features a physical comb-filter notch at $2.5\text{ kHz}$ with an electrical resonance at $3.5\text{ kHz}$. It feeds your Darkglass Anagram drive blocks with tight, controlled sub-bass and metallic high-mid clank that cuts under 7/8-string guitars without flubbing on 200+ BPM double-kick drums.
* **Bass Setting:** ABCX Blend **100% Bridge** (TWX in Dual-Coil Mode).

### 08. `prog_rick_clank` (Progressive Rock Clank)
* **Archetype:** Rickenbacker 4001/4003 Bridge with Vintage High-Pass Push-Pull
* **Coil Model:** High-output coil with $4.7\text{ nF}$ series capacitor
* **Electrical Parameters:** $L = 3.80\text{ H}$, $R_{\text{dc}} = 8.40\text{ k}\Omega$, $C_{\text{series}} = 4.7\text{ nF}$, $C_{\text{coil}} = 90\text{ pF}$
* **The Mix Role:** The series capacitor acts as a high-pass filter, stripping sub-bass mud below $150\text{ Hz}$ and aggressively spiking midrange grit ($1.5\text{--}2.5\text{ kHz}$). When pushed into a Darkglass B7K or Vintage Deluxe engine, it delivers that legendary Chris Squire (Yes) / Geddy Lee (Rush) / Justin Chancellor (Tool) pick grind.
* **Bass Setting:** ABCX Blend **100% Bridge** (TWX in Single or Dual Mode).

### 09. `power_trio_bulldozer` (Heavy Rock & Metal Solos)
* **Archetype:** Custom P/MM in Series (Sandberg / Dingwall Series)
* **Coil Model:** Split P ($4.8\text{ H}$) $+$ MM Humbucker ($2.4\text{ H}$) in series
* **Electrical Parameters:** $L = 7.20\text{ H}$, $R_{\text{dc}} = 14.10\text{ k}\Omega$, $R_{\text{eddy}} = 250\text{ k}\Omega$, $C_{\text{coil}} = 50\text{ pF}$
* **The Mix Role:** Inductive series addition produces a massive $+5.8\text{ dB}$ voltage jump with a focused $2.0\text{ kHz}$ punch. In a power trio (guitar, bass, drums), when the guitarist steps out to play a solo, this profile expands to fill the entire frequency gap so the band sounds twice as big.
* **Bass Setting:** ABCX Blend at **Center Detent (50/50)** (TWX in Dual-Coil Mode).

### 10. `stoner_doom_mudbucker` (Subterranean Fuzz Engine)
* **Archetype:** Gibson EB-0 Mudbucker / Ultra-High Inductance Dark Series
* **Coil Model:** Extreme series overwound humbucking network
* **Electrical Parameters:** $L = 14.40\text{ H}$, $R_{\text{dc}} = 27.90\text{ k}\Omega$, $R_{\text{eddy}} = 80\text{ k}\Omega$, $C_{\text{coil}} = 80\text{ pF}$
* **The Mix Role:** Resonant peak drops to a dark $1.2\text{ kHz}$, naturally rolling off all harsh treble. When downtuning to C-standard or B-standard and slamming monolithic fuzz pedals (Big Muff, Superfuzz), it generates an earthquake-level low-mid foundation with zero fizzy, bee-like high-end buzz.
* **Bass Setting:** ABCX Blend at **Center Detent (50/50)** (TWX in Dual-Coil Mode).

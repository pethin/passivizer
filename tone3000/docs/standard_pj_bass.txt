ALLOMORPH: STANDARD P/J BASS EDITION

Transform your stock Fender P/J Bass into 19 legendary active, vintage, modern, and acoustic pickup configurations.


OVERVIEW

Allomorph delivers precision digital twins of iconic pickup topologies and control harnesses engineered for Neural Amp Modeler (NAM) and the Darkglass Anagram pedalboard.

Rather than modeling an amplifier or speaker cabinet, these models capture:
• Coil inductances, eddy currents, and DC resistance
• Aperture geometry and spatial string comb-filtering
• Era-accurate pot loading and tone capacitor values
• Cable capacitance deconvolution (750 pF)
• Pure capacitive ToneStyler rotary shunts
• Active discrete preamps and EQ buffers
• Non-linear magnetic core saturation and string dynamics

Calibrated for a 34" Standard P/J Bass with stock split-coil P neck and single-coil J bridge pickups. Exact differential deconvolution neutralizes your stock pickups' loaded electrical resonance and aperture, reconstructing each target twin in real time.

QUICK INSTRUMENT SETUP

For each voicing, set your bass's physical pickup controls to the recommended bracketed setting [Parallel], [Neck], or [Bridge]:

• [Parallel]: P Vol 100% | J Vol 100% (Both Pickups Wide Open / Center Detent)
• [Neck]: P Vol 100% | J Vol 0% (Solo P Neck Split-Coil / Blend 100% Neck)
• [Bridge]: P Vol 0% | J Vol 100% (Solo J Bridge Single-Coil / Blend 100% Bridge)
• Master Tone Knob: 100% (Wide Open across all models)

Plug straight in. All impedance matching, aperture repositioning, pot loading, and active preamp EQs are handled automatically by the NAM models.


THE 19 DIGITAL TWIN VOICINGS


Jazz Bass Family

01. Modern Jazz Active [Parallel]
Sadowsky-style active 2-band buffer with cable isolation (1M in, 100 ohm out). Realigns P/J coils into a virtual 60s Jazz pair with zero cable drag (~7.8 kHz resonance). Wideband sparkle, tight low end (+4 dB @ 40 Hz), crystalline treble (+4 dB @ 4 kHz), and scooped slap snap.

02. 60s Jazz Pair [Parallel]
Transforms your P/J into authentic 1960s dual single-coils in parallel with 3 5/8" spacing, loaded by dual 250k volume pots (125k net) and a 47nF cap. Natural woody low-mid resonance (2.7 kHz peak) with iconic 1 kHz phase cancellation.

03. 60s Jazz 22nF [Parallel]
Dual single-coil response with a 22nF ToneStyler pure capacitive shunt. Eliminates wiper resistance to produce an undamped 762 Hz vocal mid bump (-3 dB @ 1225 Hz), filtering clank while preserving aggressive Jaco/Miller bridge bite with zero wiper mud.

04. Jaco Bridge Growl [Parallel]
Classic Jaco fingerstyle setting: run both pickups wide open while the model injects 55k ohms of neck wiper decoupling resistance under the hood for signature vocal growl and burp.

Music Man StingRay Family

05. StingRay Parallel [Bridge]
Parallel dual-coil humbucker centered at 66.0mm from bridge with onboard active 2-band preamp buffer (+5 dB bass @ 50 Hz, +3 dB treble @ 7 kHz). Complete cable isolation (7.2 kHz resonance), 2.5 kHz hollow mid-scoop, and signature metallic clank.

06. StingRay Series [Bridge]
Dual-coil humbucker in series (4.0H) with active 2-band preamp buffer. +2.5 dB hotter output with aggressive 2.1 kHz midrange bark and softened treble clank (StingRay 5 / Sterling series switch).


P/MM Modern Hybrids

07. Modern P∕MM Active [Parallel]
Sandberg California / Lakland 44-02 style Split-P and Music Man bridge humbucker in parallel with onboard active buffer (3.4 kHz peak). Woody P-bass weight combined with laser-focused MM slap bite.

08. P∕MM Hybrid Series [Parallel]
Compound 4-coil array: Precision split-coil and Music Man humbucker wired in series (7.2H inductance, 500k harness). Massive +5.8 dB voltage boost with a forward, muscular 1.6 kHz punch and wall-shaking low-mid authority.


Progressive & Classic Rock Legends

09. Rickenbacker 4003 [Bridge]
High-output single-coil loaded with factory 330k pots and vintage 4.7nF series high-pass cap circuit. Strips sub-bass mud below 150 Hz while focusing aggressive midrange grit and pick attack (1.5–2.5 kHz) for classic prog clank.

10. Dingwall FD3 [Bridge]
Angled bridge sweet spot (48.0mm datum) of a 34"–37" fanned-fret bass with active buffer stage and stainless roundwound physics. Extreme high wave-speed clank and FD3 dual-coil sparkle (7.3 kHz peak).

11. EB-0 Mudbucker [Neck]
Overwound sidewinder series humbucker placed at neck heel (165.0mm datum, 14.4H inductance, 500k harness). Resonant peak pulled to 1.2 kHz with steep natural acoustic rolloff. Colossal subterranean low end without top-end fizz.


Acoustic Transducers

12. Upright Piezo [Neck]
Direct bridge force transducer simulation (Underwood/Realist style on 41.5" double bass scale) with velocity-to-force integration (+6 dB/oct from 70–250 Hz), 32 Hz rumble cut, and spiral rope-core dynamics. Delivers woody pizzicato bloom and warm body resonance. Recommended to be paired with 3 Sigma Audio Upright Bass AST IRs ("Acoustic Upright Standard" folder with AST file tag) in your cabinet loader for authentic soundboard radiation.


Precision Bass & Tone Shaper Family

13. Modern P Ceramic [Neck]
Transforms your P-pickup into a high-inductance ceramic split-coil (Bartolini 8CBP spec, 4.8H) with boutique 500k pots, 22nF Orange Drop cap, and hybrid treble bleed. Tight transient attack with aggressive high-mid pick presence.

14. Vintage 62 P 22nF [Neck]
Vintage '62 split-coil with modern 22nF pure capacitive shunt. Concentrates energy into an undamped, punchy 440 Hz low-mid peak (+1.5 dB, -3 dB @ 750 Hz), eliminating fret rattle while retaining forward mix punch.

15. Vintage 62 P 47nF [Neck]
Legendary Jamerson/Pino flatwound tone: vintage Alnico split-coil with 47nF ToneStyler pure capacitive shunt and flatwound string damping. Resonant peak at 450 Hz with steep rolloff above 800 Hz for pillowy sub-bass mass.

16. Vintage 50s P 100nF [Neck]
Original 1951–1959 Fullerton factory 0.1µF (100nF) paper-in-oil spec with zero wiper damping. Massive sub-bass shelf rolloff (-3 dB @ 240 Hz) for subterranean dub and reggae thump that shakes the room without mud.


P/J Bass Family

17. Modern PJ Active [Parallel]
Compound 3-coil array: ceramic split-neck and single-bridge with active 2-band buffer (1M in, 100 ohm out). +4 dB bass boost at 40 Hz, +4 dB treble at 4 kHz, and cable isolation (7.6 kHz resonance). Aggressive slap snap with thunderous low-end weight.


Studio Buffers & Dynamics

18. Active Character
Studio-grade ultra-high-impedance active buffer twin (1M in, 100 ohm out). Deconvolves 750 pF cable capacitance and dual 250k pot damping from your stock PJ pickups, shifting resonance to 7.5–9.0 kHz for pristine pick attack, fast transients, and hi-fi air.

19. Passive Character
Authentic high-impedance passive RLC network simulation (4.2H inductance, 2.8 kHz resonance, Q = 1.4) loaded with a vintage CTS 250k harness and 750 pF cable capacitance. Stacks organic vintage warmth, tactile touch sensitivity, and harmonic bloom onto your signal chain.

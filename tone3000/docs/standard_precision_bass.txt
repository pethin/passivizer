ALLOMORPH: STANDARD PRECISION BASS EDITION

Transform your stock Fender P-Bass into 21 legendary active, vintage, modern, and acoustic pickup configurations.


OVERVIEW

Allomorph delivers precision digital twins of iconic pickup topologies and control harnesses engineered for Neural Amp Modeler (NAM) and the Darkglass Anagram pedalboard.

Rather than modeling an amplifier or speaker cabinet, these models capture:
• Coil inductances, eddy currents, and DC resistance
• Aperture geometry and spatial string comb-filtering
• Era-accurate pot loading and tone capacitor values
• Cable capacitance deconvolution (750 pF)
• Pure capacitive ToneStyler rotary shunts
• Active discrete preamps and EQ buffers
• Non-linear magnetic core saturation and string dynamics

Calibrated for a 34" Standard Precision Bass with stock split-coil pickups. Exact differential deconvolution neutralizes your stock pickup's loaded resonance and aperture, reconstructing each target twin in real time.


RECOMMENDED SIGNAL CHAIN

[Fender P-Bass]
  -> [Block 1: Allomorph NAM Preamp]  (Input / Pickup Front-End)
  -> [Block 2: Amp / Drive / Preamp]  (Darkglass B7K, SVT, B-15, etc.)
  -> [Block 3: Speaker Cabinet IR]    (8x10, 4x10, 1x15, etc.)
  -> [FOH / Audio Interface / DAW]

Why Block 1? Drive and preamp circuits react directly to pickup resonant peaks. Placing Allomorph first ensures downstream pedals distort authentic vintage or active resonant envelopes rather than an unshaped DI.


QUICK INSTRUMENT SETUP

• Physical Volume Knob: 100% (Wide Open)
• Physical Tone Knob: 100% (Wide Open)

Plug straight in. All impedance matching, aperture repositioning, pot loading, and active preamp EQs are handled automatically by the NAM models.


THE 21 DIGITAL TWIN VOICINGS


Jazz Bass Family

01. Modern Jazz Active
Sadowsky-style active 2-band buffer with cable isolation (1M in, 100 ohm out). Wideband sparkle, punchy low end (+4 dB @ 40 Hz), crystalline treble (+4 dB @ 4 kHz), and scooped slap definition.

02. 60s Jazz Pair
Dual 1960s single-coils in parallel (3 5/8" spacing) with vintage 250k pots and 47nF cap. Woody low-mid resonance (2.7 kHz peak) with classic 1 kHz phase-cancellation scoop.

03. 60s Jazz 22nF
Dual single-coils with 22nF pure capacitive shunt. Eliminates wiper mud for an undamped 762 Hz vocal mid bump, preserving aggressive Jaco/Miller bridge bite without treble clank.

04. Jaco Bridge Growl
Models decoupled dual volumes with 55k neck wiper resistance. Pulls comb notch into vocal 550–800 Hz zone for signature Jaco bridge growl while retaining low-end body.

05. 60s Jazz Bridge
Authentic 60s bridge datum (63.5mm from saddle) with 250k harness. Focused midrange bite (800 Hz–1.6 kHz) with articulate, percussive staccato finger attack.


Music Man StingRay Family

06. StingRay Parallel
Parallel dual-coil humbucker centered at 66.0mm with active 2-band preamp (+5 dB bass @ 50 Hz, +3 dB treble @ 7 kHz). Cable isolation, 2.5 kHz mid-scoop, and signature metallic clank.

07. StingRay Series
Dual-coil humbucker in series with active 2-band preamp. +2.5 dB hotter output with aggressive 2.1 kHz midrange bark and softened treble clank (StingRay 5 / Sterling series switch).


P/J Bass Family

08. Modern PJ Active
Ceramic split-neck and single-bridge with active 2-band buffer (+4 dB @ 40 Hz, +4 dB @ 4 kHz) and cable isolation (7.6 kHz peak). Modern slap snap with deep sub-bass foundation.

09. Vintage PJ Passive
1980s Fender Special / Yamaha BB style Alnico split/single pair with dual 250k volume loading (125k net). Thick hard-rock punch, woody mid bite, and authoritative low grunt.


P/MM Modern Hybrids

10. Modern P∕MM Active
Sandberg California / Lakland 44-02 style Split-P and Music Man bridge humbucker in parallel with onboard active buffer (3.4 kHz peak). Woody P-bass weight combined with laser-focused MM slap bite.

11. P∕MM Hybrid Series
Split P and MM humbucker wired in series (7.2H inductance, 500k pots). Massive +5.8 dB inductive surge with a forward, muscular 1.6 kHz punch and wall-shaking low-mids.


Progressive & Classic Rock Legends

12. Rickenbacker 4003
High-output bridge coil with factory 330k pots and vintage 4.7nF series high-pass capacitor. Cuts sub-bass mud below 150 Hz for biting prog-rock pick grit and clank.

13. Dingwall FD3
Angled bridge sweet spot (48.0mm datum) of a 34"–37" fanned-fret bass with active buffer stage and stainless roundwound physics. Extreme high wave-speed clank and FD3 dual-coil sparkle (7.3 kHz peak).

14. EB-0 Mudbucker
Overwound neck-heel sidewinder humbucker (14.4H, 500k pots). Resonant peak pulled to 1.2 kHz with steep natural acoustic rolloff for colossal subterranean low end without top-end fizz.


Acoustic Transducers

15. Upright Piezo
Direct bridge force sensor simulation (Underwood/Realist style on 41.5" scale) with velocity-to-force leaky integration (+6 dB/oct from 70–250 Hz) and 32 Hz rumble cut. Woody pizzicato bloom and warm body resonance.


Precision Bass & Tone Shaper Family
 
16. Modern P Ceramic
High-inductance ceramic split-coil (Bartolini 8CBP, 4.8H) with boutique 500k pots, 22nF Orange Drop cap, and hybrid treble bleed network. Delivers tight transient attack, aggressive high-mid pick presence, and modern high-fidelity articulation.
 
17. Vintage 62 P 22nF
Vintage '62 split-coil with modern 22nF pure capacitive shunt. Concentrates energy into an undamped, punchy 440 Hz low-mid peak (+1.5 dB, -3 dB @ 750 Hz) that cuts fret clatter while retaining forward mix punch and authoritative weight.
 
18. Vintage 62 P 47nF
Iconic Jamerson/Pino flatwound tone: vintage Alnico split-coil with 47nF ToneStyler pure capacitive shunt and flatwound string damping. Resonant 450 Hz bump with steep top-end rolloff above 800 Hz for pillowy sub-bass mass and vintage warmth.
 
19. Vintage 50s P 100nF
Original 1951–1959 Fullerton factory 0.1µF (100nF) paper-in-oil spec with zero wiper damping. Massive sub-bass shelf rolloff (-3 dB @ 240 Hz) for subterranean dub and reggae thump that shakes the foundation without mud.
 
 
Studio Buffers & Dynamics
 
20. Active Character
Studio-grade ultra-high-impedance active buffer twin (1M in, 100 ohm out). Deconvolves 750 pF cable capacitance and CTS 250k pot damping from your stock P-Bass pickup, shifting resonance to 7.5–9.0 kHz for pristine pick attack, fast transients, and hi-fi air.
 
21. Passive Character
Authentic high-impedance passive RLC network simulation (4.2H inductance, 2.8 kHz resonance, Q = 1.4) loaded with a vintage CTS 250k harness and 750 pF cable capacitance. Stacks organic vintage warmth, tactile touch sensitivity, and harmonic bloom onto your signal chain.

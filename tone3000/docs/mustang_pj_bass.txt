ALLOMORPH: MUSTANG P/J BASS EDITION

Transform your short-scale Fender Mustang P/J Bass into 22 legendary active, vintage, modern, and acoustic pickup configurations.


OVERVIEW

Allomorph delivers precision digital twins of iconic pickup topologies and control harnesses engineered for Neural Amp Modeler (NAM) and the Darkglass Anagram pedalboard.

Rather than modeling an amplifier or speaker cabinet, these models capture:
• Coil inductances, eddy currents, and DC resistance
• Aperture geometry and spatial string comb-filtering
• Era-accurate potentiometer loading and tone capacitor values
• Instrument cable capacitance drag (750 pF)
• Pure capacitive ToneStyler rotary shunts
• Active onboard discrete preamps and EQ buffers
• 30" short-scale wave speeds and string tension scaling
• Non-linear magnetic core saturation and dynamic string compliance

Calibrated for a 30" Short Scale Fender Mustang P/J Bass with stock passive split-coil P neck and single-coil J bridge pickups. Exact differential deconvolution neutralizes your stock pickups' loaded electrical resonance, 30" wave-speed envelope, and spatial aperture, reconstructing each target twin in real time.


RECOMMENDED SIGNAL CHAIN

[Mustang P/J Bass]
  -> [Block 1: Allomorph NAM Preamp]  (Input / Pickup Front-End)
  -> [Block 2: Amp / Drive / Preamp]  (Darkglass B7K, SVT, B-15, etc.)
  -> [Block 3: Speaker Cabinet IR]    (8x10, 4x10, 1x15, etc.)
  -> [FOH / Audio Interface / DAW]

Why Block 1? Drive and preamp circuits react directly to pickup resonant peaks. Placing Allomorph first ensures downstream pedals distort authentic vintage or active resonant envelopes rather than an unshaped DI.


QUICK INSTRUMENT SETUP

For each voicing, set your Mustang's 3-way toggle switch to the recommended bracketed setting [Parallel], [P-Bass], or [J-Bridge]:

• [Parallel]: Toggle Middle (Both Pickups Active)
• [P-Bass]: Toggle Up (Solo Split-P Neck Pickup)
• [J-Bridge]: Toggle Down (Solo J Bridge Single-Coil)
• Master Volume: 100% | Master Tone: 100% (Wide Open across all models)

Plug straight in. All scale length scaling, aperture repositioning, pot loading, and discrete preamp EQs are handled automatically by the NAM models.


THE 22 DIGITAL TWIN VOICINGS


Jazz Bass Family

01. Modern Active Jazz Bass (NYC 2-Band) [Parallel]
Sadowsky-style active 2-band buffer with cable isolation (1M in, 100 ohm out). Realigns Mustang P/J coils into a virtual 60s Jazz pair with zero cable drag (~7.8 kHz resonance). Wideband sparkle, tight low end (+4 dB @ 40 Hz), crystalline treble (+4 dB @ 4 kHz), and scooped slap snap.

02. Vintage '60s Jazz Bass Pair (Parallel) [Parallel]
Transforms your short scale into authentic 1960s dual single-coils in parallel with 3 5/8" spacing, loaded by dual 250k volume pots (125k net) and a 47nF cap. Natural woody low-mid resonance (2.7 kHz peak) with iconic 1 kHz phase cancellation.

03. '60s Jazz Bass Pair (22nF ToneStyler) [Parallel]
Dual single-coil response with a 22nF ToneStyler pure capacitive shunt. Eliminates wiper resistance to produce an undamped 762 Hz vocal mid bump (-3 dB @ 1225 Hz), filtering clank while preserving aggressive Jaco/Miller bridge bite with zero wiper mud.

04. '60s Jazz Bridge Growl (Jaco Bias) [Parallel]
Classic Jaco fingerstyle setting: run toggle in middle while the model injects 55k ohms of neck wiper decoupling resistance under the hood for signature vocal growl and burp.

05. '60s Jazz Bridge Single-Coil [J-Bridge]
Calibrated from your stock J-bridge single-coil and scaled to authentic 60s 34" bridge geometry. Narrow 0.75" aperture loaded by vintage 250k pots. Focused midrange harmonic bite (800 Hz–1.6 kHz) with articulate, percussive staccato finger attack.


Music Man StingRay Family

06. Music Man StingRay (Active 2-Band Parallel) [J-Bridge]
Parallel dual-coil humbucker centered at 66.0mm from bridge with onboard active 2-band preamp buffer (+5 dB bass @ 50 Hz, +3 dB treble @ 7 kHz). Complete cable isolation (7.2 kHz resonance), 2.5 kHz hollow mid-scoop, and signature metallic clank.

07. Music Man StingRay (Active 2-Band Series) [J-Bridge]
Dual-coil humbucker in series (4.0H) with active 2-band preamp buffer. +2.5 dB hotter output with aggressive 2.1 kHz midrange bark and softened treble clank (StingRay 5 / Sterling series switch).


P/MM Modern Hybrids

08. Modern Active P/MM Bass (Parallel) [Parallel]
Sandberg California / Lakland 44-02 style Split-P and Music Man bridge humbucker in parallel with onboard active buffer (3.4 kHz peak). Woody P-bass weight combined with laser-focused MM slap bite.

09. P/MM Hybrid (Series Sum) [Parallel]
Compound 4-coil array: Precision split-coil and Music Man humbucker wired in series (7.2H inductance, 500k harness). Massive +5.8 dB voltage boost with a forward, muscular 1.6 kHz punch and wall-shaking low-mid authority.


Progressive & Classic Rock Legends

10. Rickenbacker 4003 Bridge (4.7nF HPF) [J-Bridge]
High-output single-coil loaded with factory 330k pots and vintage 4.7nF series high-pass cap circuit. Strips sub-bass mud below 150 Hz while focusing aggressive midrange grit and pick attack (1.5–2.5 kHz) for classic prog clank.

11. Dingwall Fanned Multi-Scale Bridge (Active) [J-Bridge]
Angled bridge sweet spot (48.0mm datum) of a 34"–37" fanned-fret bass with active buffer stage and stainless roundwound physics. Extreme high wave-speed clank and FD3 dual-coil sparkle (7.3 kHz peak).

12. Gibson Mudbucker Ultra Series [P-Bass]
Overwound sidewinder series humbucker placed at neck heel (165.0mm datum, 14.4H inductance, 500k harness). Resonant peak pulled to 1.2 kHz with steep natural acoustic rolloff. Colossal subterranean low end without top-end fizz.


Acoustic Transducers

13. Upright Acoustic Double Bass (Bridge Piezo) [P-Bass]
Direct bridge force transducer simulation (Underwood/Realist style on 41.5" double bass scale) with velocity-to-force integration (+6 dB/oct from 70–250 Hz), 32 Hz rumble cut, and spiral rope-core dynamics. Delivers woody pizzicato bloom and warm body resonance.


Precision Bass & Tone Shaper Family

14. Vintage '62 P-Bass (Alnico V - Tone Open) [P-Bass]
Iconic 1962 Fullerton Alnico V split-coil (3.8H) with vintage CTS 250k pots and 47nF paper-in-oil cap wide open. Lower eddy damping yields organic touch sensitivity, woody bloom, and open harmonic extension (2.1 kHz loaded peak).

15. Modern Ceramic Split-P (Boutique 500k) [P-Bass]
Transforms your neck pickup into a high-inductance ceramic split-coil (Bartolini 8CBP spec, 4.8H) with boutique 500k pots, 22nF Orange Drop cap, and hybrid treble bleed. Tight transient attack with aggressive high-mid pick presence.

16. Vintage '62 Split-P (22nF ToneStyler Punch) [P-Bass]
Vintage '62 split-coil with a modern Fender-spec 22nF ToneStyler pure capacitive shunt. Concentrates energy into an undamped, punchy 440 Hz low-mid peak (+1.5 dB, -3 dB @ 750 Hz), eliminating fret rattle while retaining forward mix punch.

17. Vintage '62 Split-P Motown Thump (47nF ToneStyler Flatwound) [P-Bass]
Legendary Jamerson/Pino flatwound tone: vintage Alnico split-coil with 47nF ToneStyler pure capacitive shunt and flatwound string damping. Resonant peak at 450 Hz with steep rolloff above 800 Hz for pillowy sub-bass mass.

18. Vintage '50s Split-P Deep Dub (100nF ToneStyler Fullerton Spec) [P-Bass]
Original 1951–1959 Fullerton factory 0.1µF (100nF) paper-in-oil spec with zero wiper damping. Massive sub-bass shelf rolloff (-3 dB @ 240 Hz) for subterranean dub and reggae thump that shakes the room without mud.


P/J Bass Family

19. Modern Active P/J Bass [Parallel]
Compound 3-coil array: ceramic split-neck and single-bridge with active 2-band buffer (1M in, 100 ohm out). +4 dB bass boost at 40 Hz, +4 dB treble at 4 kHz, and cable isolation (7.6 kHz resonance). Aggressive slap snap with thunderous low-end weight.

20. Vintage '80s Passive P/J Bass [Parallel]
1980s Fender Jazz Bass Special (Duff McKagan) / Yamaha BB3000 style Alnico V split/single pair in parallel with dual 250k volume loading (125k net). Thick hard-rock punch, woody mid bite, and authoritative low-end grunt.


Studio Buffers & Dynamics

21. Modern Studio Active Buffer (Zero Cable Loading) [Parallel]
Studio-grade ultra-high-impedance active buffer twin (1M in, 100 ohm out). Deconvolves 750 pF cable capacitance and pot damping from stock Mustang pickups without changing coil positions. Shifts resonance to 7.5–9.0 kHz for pristine pick attack, fast transients, and hi-fi air.

22. Passive Character (Dynamic Baseline / True Bypass) [Parallel]
Pure passive dynamic twin with bit-exact 0.00 dB flat response (20 Hz–20 kHz). Calibrated to your stock Mustang P/J Bass's natural Alnico V dynamics and output staging. Serves as a transparent baseline reference for seamless A/B comparison and unity routing.

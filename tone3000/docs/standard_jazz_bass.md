ALLOMORPH: STANDARD JAZZ BASS EDITION

Transform your stock Fender Jazz Bass into 22 legendary active, vintage, modern, and acoustic pickup configurations.


OVERVIEW

Allomorph delivers precision digital twins of iconic pickup topologies and control harnesses engineered for Neural Amp Modeler (NAM) and the Darkglass Anagram pedalboard.

Rather than modeling an amplifier or speaker cabinet, these models capture:
• Coil inductances, eddy currents, and DC resistance
• Aperture geometry and spatial string comb-filtering
• Era-accurate pot loading and tone capacitor values
• Cable capacitance deconvolution (750 pF)
• Pure capacitive ToneStyler rotary shunts
• Active discrete preamps and EQ buffers
• Non-linear magnetic core saturation and string dynamics

Calibrated for a 34" Standard Jazz Bass with stock single-coil pickups. Exact differential deconvolution neutralizes your stock pickups' loaded electrical resonance and aperture, reconstructing each target twin in real time.

QUICK INSTRUMENT SETUP

For each voicing, set your bass's physical pickup controls to the recommended bracketed setting [Parallel], [Neck], or [Bridge]:

• [Parallel]: Neck Vol 100% | Bridge Vol 100% (Both Pickups Wide Open)
• [Neck]: Neck Vol 100% | Bridge Vol 0% (Solo Neck Pickup)
• [Bridge]: Neck Vol 0% | Bridge Vol 100% (Solo Bridge Pickup)
• Master Tone Knob: 100% (Wide Open across all models)

Plug straight in. All impedance matching, aperture repositioning, pot loading, and active preamp EQs are handled automatically by the NAM models.


THE 22 DIGITAL TWIN VOICINGS


Jazz Bass Family

01. Modern Active Jazz Bass (NYC 2-Band) [Parallel]
Sadowsky-style active 2-band buffer with cable isolation (1M in, 100 ohm out). Wideband sparkle, punchy low end (+4 dB @ 40 Hz), crystalline treble (+4 dB @ 4 kHz), and scooped slap definition.

02. Vintage '60s Jazz Bass Pair (Parallel) [Parallel]
Dual 1960s single-coils in parallel (3 5/8" spacing) with vintage 250k pots and 47nF cap. Woody low-mid resonance (2.7 kHz peak) with classic 1 kHz phase-cancellation scoop.

03. '60s Jazz Bass Pair (22nF ToneStyler) [Parallel]
Dual single-coils with 22nF pure capacitive shunt. Eliminates wiper mud for an undamped 762 Hz vocal mid bump, preserving aggressive Jaco/Miller bridge bite without treble clank.

04. '60s Jazz Bridge Growl (Jaco Bias) [Parallel]
Classic Jaco fingerstyle setting: run both pickups wide open while the model injects 55k ohms of neck wiper decoupling resistance under the hood for signature vocal growl and burp.

05. '60s Jazz Bridge Single-Coil [Bridge]
Authentic 60s bridge datum (63.5mm from saddle) with 250k harness. Focused midrange bite (800 Hz–1.6 kHz) with articulate, percussive staccato finger attack.


Precision Bass & Tone Shaper Family

06. Modern Ceramic Split-P (Boutique 500k) [Neck]
Transforms your neck pickup into a high-inductance ceramic split-coil (Bartolini 8CBP, 4.8H) with boutique 500k pots, 22nF cap, and treble bleed. Tight transient attack with aggressive high-mid presence.

07. Vintage '62 P-Bass (Alnico V - Tone Open) [Neck]
Classic 1962 CTS 250k harness with 47nF paper-in-oil cap wide open. Lower eddy damping yields organic touch sensitivity, woody bloom, and open harmonic extension (2.1 kHz peak).

08. Vintage '62 Split-P (22nF ToneStyler) [Neck]
Vintage '62 split-coil with modern 22nF pure capacitive shunt. Punchy 440 Hz low-mid peak (+1.5 dB, -3 dB @ 750 Hz) that cuts fret clatter while retaining forward mix punch.

09. Vintage '62 Split-P Motown Thump (47nF ToneStyler) [Neck]
Iconic Jamerson/Pino flatwound tone: 47nF pure capacitive shunt with flatwound string damping. Resonant 450 Hz bump with steep top-end rolloff for pillowy sub-bass mass.

10. Vintage '50s Split-P Deep Dub (100nF ToneStyler) [Neck]
Fullerton factory 0.1µF spec with zero wiper damping. Massive sub-bass shelf rolloff (-3 dB @ 240 Hz) for subterranean dub and reggae thump that shakes the room.


P/J & Music Man Family

11. Modern Active P/J Bass [Parallel]
Ceramic split-neck and single-bridge with active 2-band buffer (+4 dB @ 40 Hz, +4 dB @ 4 kHz) and cable isolation (7.6 kHz peak). Modern slap snap with deep sub-bass foundation.

12. Vintage '80s Passive P/J Bass [Parallel]
1980s Fender Special / Yamaha BB style Alnico split/single pair with dual 250k volume loading (125k net). Thick hard-rock punch, woody mid bite, and authoritative low grunt.

13. Music Man StingRay (Active 2-Band Parallel) [Bridge]
Parallel dual-coil humbucker centered at 66.0mm with active 2-band preamp (+5 dB bass @ 50 Hz, +3 dB treble @ 7 kHz). Cable isolation, 2.5 kHz mid-scoop, and signature metallic clank.

14. Music Man StingRay (Active 2-Band Series) [Bridge]
Dual-coil humbucker in series with active 2-band preamp. +2.5 dB hotter output with aggressive 2.1 kHz midrange bark and softened treble clank (StingRay 5 / Sterling series switch).


Progressive, Rock & Acoustic Transducers

15. Rickenbacker 4003 Bridge (4.7nF HPF) [Bridge]
High-output bridge coil with factory 330k pots and vintage 4.7nF series high-pass capacitor. Cuts sub-bass mud below 150 Hz for biting prog-rock pick grit and clank.

16. Modern Active P/MM Bass (Parallel) [Parallel]
Sandberg California / Lakland 44-02 style Split-P and Music Man bridge humbucker in parallel with onboard active buffer (3.4 kHz peak). Woody P-bass weight combined with laser-focused MM slap bite.

17. P/MM Hybrid (Series Sum) [Parallel]
Split P and MM humbucker wired in series (7.2H inductance, 500k pots). Massive +5.8 dB inductive surge with a forward, muscular 1.6 kHz punch and wall-shaking low-mids.

18. Gibson Mudbucker Ultra Series [Neck]
Overwound neck-heel sidewinder humbucker (14.4H, 500k pots). Resonant peak pulled to 1.2 kHz with steep natural acoustic rolloff for colossal subterranean low end without top-end fizz.

19. Dingwall Fanned Multi-Scale Bridge (Active) [Bridge]
Angled bridge sweet spot (48.0mm datum) of a 34"–37" fanned-fret bass with active buffer stage and stainless roundwound physics. Extreme high wave-speed clank and FD3 dual-coil sparkle (7.3 kHz peak).

20. Upright Acoustic Double Bass (Bridge Piezo) [Neck]
Direct bridge force sensor simulation (Underwood/Realist style on 41.5" scale) with velocity-to-force leaky integration (+6 dB/oct from 70–250 Hz) and 32 Hz rumble cut. Woody pizzicato bloom and warm body resonance.


Studio Buffers & Dynamics

21. Modern Studio Active Buffer (Zero Cable Loading) [Parallel]
Studio 1M ohm active buffer twin. Deconvolves 750 pF cable capacitance and dual 250k pot damping from your stock Jazz pickups, shifting resonance to 7.5–9.0 kHz for pristine pick attack and hi-fi air.

22. Passive Character (Dynamic Baseline / True Bypass) [Parallel]
Bit-exact 0.00 dB flat linear response calibrated to stock Jazz Bass Alnico V dynamics and output staging. Serves as a transparent reference for seamless A/B comparison and unity routing.
